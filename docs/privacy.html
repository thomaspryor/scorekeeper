Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy covers the Scorekeep app for iPhone and iPad and the Scorekeep website at scorekeep.co.

We don't collect your data

Scorekeep does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Player names, colors, scores, settings, and saved games are stored only on your device (in the app's local storage on iPhone and iPad, or your browser's local storage on the website). They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

To delete this data, delete the app, or clear the website's data in your browser settings. On iPhone and iPad, saved games are also removed when you delete them in the app.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. The website is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes standard request information (such as IP address) to deliver the page. We do not use that information.

Children

Scorekeep is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
