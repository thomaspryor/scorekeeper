Scorekeep keeps score for any game, so you can stop hunting for a pen and paper. Board games, card games, darts, trivia night, backyard games: if it has points, Scorekeep can track it.

Add your players, give each one a color, and tap plus or minus to change scores. Hold a button to count quickly. Big, bright rows are easy to read from across the table.

SCORING
- Tap to add or subtract points, hold for rapid counting
- Set the step to 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100 for high-scoring games
- A running total shows how many points you just added
- Undo any mistake with the Undo button or a quick shake

GAME TOOLS
- Sort players by score, highest or lowest first
- Lowest-score-wins games (golf, hearts) are supported: sort low to high before ending
- End the game to crown the winner with confetti and final standings
- Rematch keeps the same players and starts the scores over
- Lighthearted trash talk reacts to the standings as you play

YOUR GAMES
- Save finished games and reload them later
- Drag to reorder players, swipe to edit or delete
- Works offline with no account and no sign-up

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your device. Scorekeep has no ads, no tracking and no account, and it collects no data.
